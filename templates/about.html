About

The Seventh-day Adventist Hospital in Asamang, Ghana, is a mission-based healthcare facility located in the Sekyere South District. As part of the Ghana Adventist Health Service, we operate under the Christian Health Association of Ghana (CHAG), an agency of the Ministry of Health.

Since our establishment in 1984, we have proudly served as the premier hospital in the Adventist Network. Our facility stands as the primary referral center for the district, offering comprehensive healthcare services that include:

  • Eye Care & Ophthalmology
  • Dental Services
  • ENT (Ear, Nose, and Throat) Care
  • Gynecological Services
  • General Surgery
  • Pediatric Care
  • Psychiatric Services
  • Reproductive and Child Health (RCH)
  • Advanced Diagnostic Imaging (X-Ray)
  • And many other specialized services

Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our team of over 300 dedicated healthcare professionals, including specialists, nurses, and paramedics, supported by more than 90 skilled support staff. This experienced team works together to provide the highest standard of medical care, making us a true center of healthcare excellence in the region.
