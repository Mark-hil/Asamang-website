Our Heritage & Mission

A Pillar of Health, Hope, and Healing in Ghana

The Seventh-day Adventist Hospital in Asamang was founded in 1984 with a singular vision: to bring Christ-centered, high-quality medical care to the people of the Sekyere South District and surrounding communities.

As an integral member of the Ghana Adventist Health Service (GAHS) operating under the Christian Health Association of Ghana (CHAG) and the Ministry of Health, we serve as the primary district referral hospital.
