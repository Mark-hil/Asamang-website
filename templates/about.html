About

The Seventh day Adventist Hospital - Asamang (Ghana) is a mission based health facility at Asamang in the Sekyere South District of Ghana. It's regulated by the Ghana Adventist Health Service, a subsidiary of the Christian Health Association of Ghana (CHAG) ; an agency under the Ministry of Health. The facility serves as the referral center for the District and offers all basic and advanced services like Eye, Dental, ENT, Gynaecological, General Surgery, Paediatric, Psychiatry, RCH, X-Ray, Diagnostic Services among others. It has been in existence since 1984's being the Premier Hospital of the Adventist Network. It prides itself as the medical center of excellence and rightly so evidenced by the services they provide. Currently, it operates with over 300 health professionals including Specialists, Nurses and other paramedics, as well as over 90 other supporting health workers.
