Our standard inpatient visiting hours are divided into three daily sessions:
  • Morning: 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM
  • Afternoon: 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM
  • Evening: 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Exceptions can be arranged for critically ill patients or pediatric wards under the guidance of the attending nursing in-charge.

Yes, Asamang SDA Hospital is fully accredited under CHAG and accepts the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) for consultations, diagnostics, medications, and surgical procedures covered by the benefit package. We also partner with leading private health insurance plans in Ghana.

For emergency and urgent care, no appointment is required — our Accident & Emergency triage is open 24/7 without pause. For specialized clinics (Cardiology, Surgery, ENT, Eye, Dental, and OB/GYN), we encourage you to book online or call in advance to ensure specialist availability and minimal waiting time.

Please bring a valid national identification (Ghana Card), your active NHIS card or private health insurance card, any previous medical documents, laboratory reports, or X-rays, and your current prescription medications.

Our standby ambulance is equipped with resuscitation gear and trauma support. To request emergency ambulance transport, dial our 24/7 emergency hotline at +233 531 047 199.

Yes, our licensed hospital pharmacy and dispensary operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, ensuring immediate availability of essential and emergency medications.
