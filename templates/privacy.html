Patient Privacy Policy

How Asamang SDA Hospital collects, handles, stores, and protects your confidential personal health records and digital appointment data.

1. Our Commitment to Patient Privacy

At Asamang Seventh-day Adventist Hospital, we regard medical confidentiality as sacred. We adhere strictly to ethical medical codes and the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Republic of Ghana to ensure that your consultations, diagnosis, lab results, and personal identity remain secure.

2. Information We Collect

To provide safe, comprehensive, and tailored clinical care, our healthcare professionals collect:

Personal Identification
  • Full Legal Name & Date of Birth
  • Ghana Card / Passport Number
  • Contact Phone & Residential Address
  • Next-of-Kin Emergency Contacts
Clinical & Health Data
  • Medical History & Allergies
  • Physician Diagnostic Notes
  • Lab & Imaging Results (X-Ray, Scans)
  • Prescriptions & Surgical Records

3. How We Use Your Information

Your health data is used exclusively to facilitate your care journey:

  • Clinical Coordination: Enabling seamless collaboration between triage, doctors, laboratories, operating theaters, and pharmacy.
  • Insurance Claims Processing: Verifying coverage and processing claims with the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) or accredited private health insurers.
  • Appointment Reminders: Notifying you of scheduled doctor visits, lab follow-ups, or prescription refills.

4. Data Security & Storage Safeguards

We maintain multi-layered physical, technical, and administrative controls to protect patient folders and digital hospital databases against unauthorized access or loss.

Role-Based Access

Only medical staff directly assigned to your care have permission to review your medical folder.

Encrypted Infrastructure

Digital records, appointment data, and backups are stored on encrypted, high-availability servers.

5. Website Cookies & Session Data

Our website utilizes only standard session cookies strictly necessary to process online appointment submissions and maintain secure staff logins. We do not sell, rent, or trade patient data to third-party advertisers.

6. Your Rights Under Ghana Law

Under Act 843, you have the right to request access to your medical history, update personal details, or request an official medical summary for specialist referral. Contact our Records Department with valid national identification to initiate a request.
